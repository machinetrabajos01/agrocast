Problem and context

Fertilizers and pesticides are a large share of crop production costs in Colombia, and most of them are imported and priced in dollars. Between January 2021 and mid-2022 the official agricultural input price index rose from 100 to more than 220 points, and it fell again during 2023. Producers who buy inputs once or twice per season cannot easily tell whether to buy now or wait, and budgets prepared months in advance become outdated.

AGROCAST forecasts the official UPRA input price index one to six months ahead and turns the forecast into a purchase recommendation for each purchase plan.

Questions the model answers

  1. How much will the input price index change in the next one to six months?
  2. Is the expected change large enough (more than ±3%) to move the purchase date of a plan?
  3. How uncertain is the forecast (80% and 95% ranges)?

Learning task

Supervised regression on a time series (multi-step forecasting). Monthly frequency, horizon of six months, target = index level (January 2021 = 100). Each forecast uses only information available at its origin month.

Quality, relevance and sufficiency

  • Completeness: {{ quality.meses_ausentes }} missing months and {{ quality.faltantes_indice_total }} missing values in the target.
  • Consistency: the annex and the Datos Abiertos API agree in {{ quality.comparacion_datos_abiertos.meses_comparados }} months (maximum difference {{ quality.comparacion_datos_abiertos.diferencia_maxima }} points, rounding).
  • Relevance: the 12-month change of an index rebuilt from SIPSA-I prices correlates with the UPRA sub-indices: fertilizers {{ quality.correlacion_variacion_12m_sipsa_vs_upra.Fertilizantes }}, herbicides {{ quality.correlacion_variacion_12m_sipsa_vs_upra.Herbicidas }}, fungicides {{ quality.correlacion_variacion_12m_sipsa_vs_upra.Fungicidas }}, insecticides {{ quality.correlacion_variacion_12m_sipsa_vs_upra.Insecticidas }}.
  • Basket changes: January 2024 ({{ quality.cambios_de_canasta.ene_2024_amplia_canasta.variacion_mes_pct }}%, robust z {{ quality.cambios_de_canasta.ene_2024_amplia_canasta.z_robusto }}) and September 2024 ({{ quality.cambios_de_canasta.sep_2024_excluye_fipronil.variacion_mes_pct }}%, z {{ quality.cambios_de_canasta.sep_2024_excluye_fipronil.z_robusto }}) are not atypical, so no splicing is needed.
  • Sufficiency: {{ quality.observaciones }} observations is a short series. It supports parsimonious models (ETS, SARIMA, regularized regression) but not complex models with many parameters.

Feasibility, limitations and risks

Risk or limitationTreatment
Short series (93 months)Parsimonious models, regularization, rolling-origin evaluation
International shocks (2021-2022)Kept in the data; intervals calibrated with historical errors
Information leakageEach origin only sees past data; automated tests
Publication lag (about 6-8 weeks)Forecast origin is the last official month; the system registers new months
Index is not a transaction priceWarnings in the platform; product prices from SIPSA-I

Technical feasibility: open official data, Python libraries, free hosting. Organizational feasibility: the decision (buy now or later) is already made by producers; the system only adds an evidence-based signal.
