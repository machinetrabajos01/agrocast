Missing values, duplicates, inconsistencies and outliers

  • Missing values: zeros in the annex mean that a sub-index did not exist yet or a product left the basket ("Other inputs" starts in {{ quality.primer_mes_otros_insumos }}, chlorpyrifos ends in {{ quality.ultimo_mes_clorpirifos }}, fipronil in {{ quality.ultimo_mes_fipronil }}). They are converted to missing values, never imputed. The target has no missing values.
  • Duplicates: none found; the loader keeps one row per month.
  • Inconsistencies: annex vs Datos Abiertos difference of at most {{ quality.comparacion_datos_abiertos.diferencia_maxima }} points; months missing from the annex are added from the API when available.
  • Outliers: {{ d.outliers|length }} months have a robust z-score above 3 in the monthly log change. They are kept because they are real market movements (2021-2022 international shock and April-May 2026).
