How domain knowledge informed the modeling

  • Imported inputs: most fertilizers and active ingredients are imported, so the exchange rate and international urea and DAP prices were tested as predictors (Ridge).
  • Prices move in proportions: the models work on the logarithm, so a 10% rise means the same at 100 or at 200 points.
  • Purchase cycle: producers buy one or two seasons ahead, which sets the horizon at six months.
  • Seasonality: input demand follows the planting seasons, so seasonal terms were offered to SARIMA and Ridge; the data did not support them.
  • Shocks are real: the 2021-2022 peak (fertilizer crisis) was kept, because removing it would make the model overconfident.
  • Decision band: a change of ±3% in six months was chosen as the minimum that justifies moving a purchase, considering storage and financing costs.
