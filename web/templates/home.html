Agricultural input purchase planning · CRISP-ML(Q)

Plan purchases.
Anticipate prices.

AGROCAST is a web information system for producers and technical teams in Colombia. It manages farms and input purchase plans, evaluates each plan with the SARIMA model that forecasts the official agricultural input price index six months ahead, and prices shopping lists with the daily DANE wholesale prices of 375 foods and their three-month outlook.
