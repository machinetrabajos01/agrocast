1. Purpose of the service

AGROCAST is a free academic tool that helps plan the purchase of agricultural inputs and food. It shows official prices published by DANE (SIPSA) and UPRA, and forecasts produced by statistical models.

2. Nature of the information

  • Prices are wholesale prices reported by DANE for specific markets and dates; retail prices are usually higher and vary between stores.
  • Forecasts and recommendations ("Buy now", "Wait", "Defer") are estimates with uncertainty. They are a decision aid, not financial or commercial advice, and do not guarantee any future price.
  • Data depend on the publication of the official sources; a product may not have a recent price on a given day.

3. Accounts

You must provide true data, keep your password confidential and report any unauthorized use through Support & Feedback. Self-registration creates producer accounts; analyst and administrator roles are assigned by an administrator.
