How to read this result

Meaning: the model forecasts the {{ o.series_label|lower }} (UPRA, January 2021 = 100) {{ o.horizon }} month(s) after the latest official month. The change is applied to today's SIPSA-I prices to estimate the cost of the plan in the purchase month.
Practical use: rises of 3% or more suggest buying in advance; falls of 3% or more suggest deferring; smaller changes keep the original date.
Limitations: the index is a national average, not the price of a specific shop or region. It does not anticipate sudden shocks (exchange rate, international urea prices, regulations) and the interval widens with the horizon. In the final test block SARIMA did not beat the persistence baseline, so the recommendation supports, but does not replace, supplier quotes.
