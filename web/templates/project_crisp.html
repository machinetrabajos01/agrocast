Quality-first machine learning

CRISP-ML(Q)

The project follows a structured lifecycle in which business context, data quality, modeling, evaluation, deployment and monitoring remain connected. Open each phase to see the actual work, decisions, figures and results.

Business & Data

Define decisions, target, sources and risks.

Preparation

Clean, align and validate temporal data.

Modeling

Train candidates and references.

Evaluation

Use rolling origins and final holdout.

Deployment

Expose evidence through the platform.

Monitoring

Track new data and forecast errors.

Quality gates

  • No future information is used to construct a historical forecast origin.
  • The final test block remains separate from model selection.
  • MAE, RMSE and MASE are reported by horizon.
  • The platform communicates uncertainty instead of hiding it.

Traceability

The repository keeps raw and processed data, source code, reports, figures, documentation and model results in separate layers.
