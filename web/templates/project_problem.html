Problem & Objectives

The project frames agricultural price forecasting as a decision-support problem rather than a promise of exact future prices.

The problem

Price uncertainty complicates planning

Fertilizers and crop-protection inputs represent important production costs. When prices are difficult to anticipate, producers face uncertainty when preparing budgets, scheduling purchases and estimating margins.

Guiding question: what performance can statistical and machine-learning models achieve when forecasting the national agricultural input price index six months ahead?
Business objectives
Forecast

Produce a six-month signal for the national index.

Compare

Evaluate ETS, SARIMA and Ridge against references.

Support

Translate results into planning-oriented views.

Document

Make the methodology reproducible and explainable.

Scope and limitations

The index is an aggregate national signal. It is not a guaranteed quote for an individual producer, region, crop or commercial presentation. Forecast intervals communicate uncertainty, and model performance is assessed through temporal backtesting.
